I recently decided to go back to school and start a new degree. That means balancing work, studying, training, and everything else life throws at me.

That pushed me to rethink how I use personal AI agents.

## From Nineth to a team

A few days ago I shared **Nineth**, my personal assistant. This grows from there, just a little bit.

Instead of building isolated bots, I'm building a team: one agent for my calendar, one for academics, one for tasks, one for training, and a main agent coordinating everything.

For this new chapter the name is **Mira Team** 🌸

## Who's on the team

![Diagram of the Mira personal agents team: Luis proposes and approves, talks to each agent in direct chats or in a study group, and each agent connects to its own source: calendars, the academic platform, a task list, or a training plan](/images/mira-team.jpg)

**Mira** is the main agent, the one coordinating everything. The 08:00 briefing in the corner of the diagram is just an example meeting.

Then there are four agents, each one tied to its own source:

- **calendar-observer** looks at my calendars for free spaces and events, and can create or move events. It works with real data.
- **platform-reader** reads my academic platform: courses, deadlines, grades, and announcements. Also real data.
- **task-prioritizer** prioritizes with a macro view and my weekly plan, and keeps my task list synced as a Q1 to Q4 matrix.
- **Hy** (coach-entreno in the diagram) tracks my progress in strength, cardio, and conditioning, connected to my training plan with its workouts and sessions.

## Two ways to talk to them

**Direct chats.** Each agent is an independent contact. I write, they write back, like a person.

**Study group.** task-prioritizer, platform-reader, and calendar-observer share a group where they work together. task-prioritizer handles the coordination, platform-reader brings the deadlines, and calendar-observer finds the free spaces.

Hy stays out of that group. It only does training, no studying.

## What planning a week looks like

- I say "do this next week", and task-prioritizer mentions its teammates.
- They publish the plan and wait for my OK. Then calendar-observer creates the blocks on my calendar.

I propose and approve. The agents do the coordinating.

## Why a team

The goal isn't to have more AI. It's to have AI that works together, understands context, and helps turn a messy week into a coordinated one.

This is the architecture I'm building around my own needs, and I'm genuinely excited to see how far it can go.
